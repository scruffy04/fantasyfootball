Draft Strategy

  • Late rounds: prioritize SLEEPER-tagged players with a realistic path to a much larger role; favor contingent RB upside when values are similar.
  • Callout system: SLEEPER = late upside; VALUE / OVERPRICED = draft-room price versus expert consensus; RISER/WATCH/FADE/PUP/OUT = current STATUS. Personal pick-window targets are highlighted in blue on the Draft Board.
  • Rounds 1–8: Build the offensive core with five or six RB/WR players, one QB and one TE.
  • Rounds 9–12: Continue RB/WR depth; take an elite DL only at clear value.
  • Rounds 12–16: Draft one LB, one DL and one DB.
  • Final round: Draft one kicker.
